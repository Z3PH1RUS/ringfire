HOW TO PLAY

You command RF-1, last dart of the inner worlds. The Mandate — seated on Titan, moon of Saturn — occupies the outer system. Retake every planet. Moons are bases and army wells; they do not have to fall for victory, but they will decide the war.

FLIGHT

  • ← → or A D rotate. Ship flies along its nose at current warp.
  • 1–9 set warp. +/- nudge. Higher warp burns fuel hard and turns slower.
  • Fuel 0 = impulse crawl (warp locked 0) until you refuel in friendly orbit, not under fire.

COMBAT

  • SPACE photon torpedo. Travels, can be dodged. Limited in flight (6). Hits ships or bombards a hostile disc.
  • X or G detonate your own photons early — area damage (clears an orbit pile).
  • F or C (or right-click) phasers: cone in front, instant hit, costs fuel.
  • H hyperjump: short hop along your nose, high fuel, brief invulnerability. Lead or detonate the hop.
  • T tracking missile (SOL only, limited, homing). Restocks slowly; faster in friendly orbit.
  • S shields. Absorb hits, drain extra fuel. Hull 0 = you die.
  • Death: marines aboard are lost forever. You respawn at a friendly world you still own (empty hold — first launch starts with 20 marines).

CONQUEST

  • B beam (hold to keep transferring). In orbit near a friendly world: beam up marines from the planet garrison onto your ship (a minimum garrison stays). In orbit near a hostile world: beam down marines from your ship to invade.
  • Bombard first — torpedoes that strike a hostile world cut defenses, then garrison.
  • Hostile guns fire on ships in orbit. Suppress them. Then beam down marines until 40 attacking marines are on the world (or wipe the garrison).
  • Ground war resolves over seconds. At 40 marines on a hostile disc, the world flips to your side.
  • The Mandate also invades. Defend weakly held inner worlds.

SHIP SYSTEMS

  • Refuel is automatic in safe friendly orbit (Class-M in GALAXY is much faster). U extra dump. R repair hull in friendly orbit.
  • Fuel regenerates slowly in space. Warp, phasers, photons, and hops all spend it. Impulse crawl at 0.
  • [ ] or , . radar zoom. M strategic map (SOL: action slows; GALAXY: time keeps running for everyone).
  • Click a body (or nearest is shown) for the info sheet — owner, marines, guns, a real fact.
  • P or ESC pause (SOL). N mute all audio (music + SFX). K Bubble (optional bad advice; never blocks you).
  • Approach a planet and the camera zooms in 30% with your ship centered, then eases back out.
  • Continuous cinematic space music (procedural Web Audio — no copied tracks). Starts on your first click or keypress. Warp and hyper-hop add engine whoosh SFX.
  • SCORE is always on the HUD: 1 point per enemy ship. Every 10 points: +10% energy (fuel) and +10% shields, up to 50% at 50 kills (TIER T1–T5). At 50 / 100 / … kills a larger BOSS ship appears (40% stronger).

CHAOS CIRCUIT

  • Rift surges — glowing zones in open space (not on planets). Fly through for a ~9s buff (one at a time): CRIMSON OVERDRIVE (faster guns), CYAN PHANTOM (enemies track worse), or GOLD SIPHON (slow fuel regen).
  • Kill streak — ship kills within ~6s stack a STREAK counter. At x3+: brief speed boost and small bonus score.
  • Salvage pods — ~half of destroyed ships drop loot. Scoop for fuel, marines, or shields. GALAXY: host runs rifts/pods/streaks for everyone.

GALAXY (INTERNET ROOMS)

  • Open https://z3ph1rus.github.io/ringfire/ on EACH COMPUTER. One player CREATE ROOM and reads the 5-character code (shown large). The other JOIN ROOM with that code.
  • Two computers, one room. Two tabs still work as a test. The host browser runs the match; closing the host tab/computer ends it.
  • Pick a name and a race (required). Four teams: Helios (gold, medium), Veil (green, slow/heavy), Spark (cyan, fast/light), Mandate (amber, medium-plus guns).
  • Multiple humans may share a race — it is a team. Host starts the match. Optional “fill empty races with AI”. Cap 12.
  • 25 worlds, homes in the four corners (3 planets each, 50 armies). Two neutrals on each edge, five in the center (25 self-ruled armies).
  • Bomb, then beam down. Own all 25 to win. A race with zero planets cannot respawn.
  • IFF is color. Keyboard-first. Enter short room chat.
  • Optional LAN fallback: python3 server.py on one machine, then everyone opens the printed Wi-Fi URL instead of a room code.

VICTORY / DEFEAT

  • WIN: all nine planets are friendly (Mercury through Pluto). Fastest time → hall of fame.
  • LOSE: no friendly planets remain — nowhere to respawn. Earth falling hurts; it is not automatic defeat.

A skilled SOL run should be possible in 10–20 minutes. Take a moon, suppress guns, beam down marines, move on. RF-1 launches with 140 fuel and 20 marines.
